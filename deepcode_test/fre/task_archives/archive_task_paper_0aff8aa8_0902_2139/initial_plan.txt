  paper_info:
    title: "RICE: Refining with Explanation for Reinforcement Learning Agents"
    core_contribution: |
      A post-hoc policy-improvement framework that (1) trains a lightweight mask
      network to identify critical decision steps of a pre-trained RL agent,
      (2) refines the agent by restarting episodes from a mixed initial-state
      distribution (default resets + sampled critical states), and
      (3) adds a Random Network Distillation (RND) exploration bonus during
      refinement. The method is evaluated on MuJoCo (dense/sparse), selfish
      mining, CAGE Challenge 2, autonomous driving (MetaDrive), and malware
      mutation.

  implementation_components: |
    1. Target-agent training
       - Train a base policy π for every task before explanation/refinement.
       - MuJoCo (Hopper-v3, Walker2d-v3, Reacher-v2, HalfCheetah-v3): use
         Stable-Baselines3 PPO with default MLP policy. Normalize observations
         for Walker2d-v3 and HalfCheetah-v3.
       - Sparse MuJoCo variants: use the sparse reward definitions from
         Mazoure et al. (2019): reward only if x > 0.6 for Hopper/Walker2d,
         x > 5 for HalfCheetah.
       - Selfish mining: train PPO in the blockchain simulator of Bar-Zur et al.
         Network: 4-layer MLP [128,128,128,128]. Actions: Adopt(l), Reveal(l),
         Mine. Whale tx fee = 10 with prob 0.01; normal tx fee = 1.
       - CAGE Challenge 2: train a PPO blue agent against the red agent
         "B-line" using the champion scheme. Combine trials of length 30, 50,
         100; final reward = average reward across the three lengths.
       - Autonomous driving: MetaDrive "Macro-v1". Train PPO via DI-drive.
         Action a ∈ [-1,1]^2 is converted to steering/acceleration/brake.
       - Malware mutation: MalConv gym from malware_rl. Train PPO via Tianshou.
         Input = malware feature vector; action space = 16 mutation actions
         (Table 2). Max 10 steps; reward = sc_0 - sc_t or +10 on evasion.

    2. Mask network (explanation module)
       - Architecture: same MLP as the target policy (for fair comparison with
         StateMask). Output a single logit passed through a sigmoid ξ(s) ∈ (0,1).
       - Interpretation: ξ(s) = P(step is critical | s). The perturbed policy is
           \bar{π}(·|s) = ξ(s) π(·|s) + (1-ξ(s)) π^r(·|s),
         where π^r is a uniform random policy over the action space.
       - The mask approximates Q_diff(s,a) = Q^π(s,a) - E_{a'}[Q^π(s,a')].
         High ξ means the current (s,a) is critical; low ξ means randomizing the
         action does not hurt performance.
       - Training environment: wrap the original env so that at each step the
         mask chooses a^e ∈ {0,1}; if a^e=0 the agent's action is executed,
         otherwise a random action is executed. The mask is trained with PPO.
       - Objective: maximize the return of the perturbed policy plus an
         intrinsic blinding bonus:
           r_mask_t = r_env(s_t,a_t) + α * (1 - ξ(s_t)).
         α controls the trade-off between explanation fidelity and sparsity.
         Use the α values in Table 3 (default 1e-4; the ablation tests
         {0.01,0.001,0.0001}).
       - After training, collect N trajectories with the target policy and
         record ξ(s_t). Critical states are those with high ξ (e.g. top-p
         percentile or ξ > τ). Store them in a replay buffer.

    3. Refinement module
       - Mixed initial-state distribution: at the start of each training
         episode, with probability p sample an initial state from the critical
         buffer and restore the simulator to that state; otherwise perform a
         normal env.reset(). Formally,
           μ(s) = p · Uniform(critical_states) + (1-p) · ρ(s).
         p is task-specific (Table 3: 0.25 or 0.50).
       - Exploration bonus: add a Random Network Distillation (RND) bonus to
         the environment reward:
           r'_t = r_t + λ · b_RND(s_t),
           b_RND(s) = || f_target(s) - f_predictor(s) ||^2,
         normalized by a running mean/std. λ is task-specific (Table 3:
         0.001 or 0.01).
       - Train a new/refined policy π' with PPO on the mixed-reset, RND-bonus
         environment. Keep the target policy frozen as the starting point or
         initialize π' from π and continue training with the modified reward.
       - For environments where direct state restoration is unavailable, fall
         back to goal-conditioned rollouts or use the action sequence that led
         to the critical state.

    4. Baselines
       - StateMask: use the official implementation; train its mask network and
         apply its refining stage (StateMask-R).
       - Jump-Start RL (JSRL): initialize refinement from demonstrations or
         high-return trajectories.
       - Self-Imitation Learning (SIL): prioritize past successful experiences
         in the replay buffer.
       - PPO fine-tuning: continue training π with a lower learning rate.
       - Alternative explanation methods: Random explanation, Integrated
         Gradients, AIRS. Fix the refining pipeline and swap the explanation
         source.

    5. Evaluation metrics
       - Episode return / success rate (evasion probability for malware,
         collision/destination success for driving, mining revenue for selfish
         mining, negative reward for CAGE).
       - Fidelity score: correlation between mask importance and the actual
         performance drop when the action at that step is randomized. Higher
         is better.
       - Training time of the mask network (seconds for a fixed sample budget).
       - Action-frequency histograms for the malware case study.

    6. Key formulas to implement
       - Perturbed policy mixture:
           \bar{π}(a|s) = ξ(s)π(a|s) + (1-ξ(s))π^r(a|s).
       - Mask-network reward:
           r_mask = r_env + α(1-ξ(s)).
       - RND bonus:
           b_RND(s) = ||φ_target(s) - φ_pred(s)||^2.
       - Mixed reset:
           s_0 ~ μ  with  P(critical)=p,  P(default)=1-p.
       - Refining reward:
           r_refine = r_env + λ b_RND.
       - Theoretical bound (Theorem 3.6):
           V^{π*}(ρ) - V^{π'}(ρ) ≤ O( ε/(1-γ)^2 · || d_ρ^{π*} / d_ρ^{\hat{π}} ||_∞ ).
